OFFICIAL PROGRAM OPERATION

How the Empowerment Scholarship Account (ESA) Program Works

The Arizona Empowerment Scholarship Account (ESA) program enables parents to opt out of the public school district or charter system and receive dedicated state funds directly to customize their children's education through qualified educational providers, individualized curricula, and accredited instructional materials.

🏛️

Administering Agency

Arizona Department of Education (ADE). Governed by: Arizona Revised Statutes Title 15 (Universal ESA Expansion).

Visit Official State Portal ↗
💳

Digital Platform

Arizona utilizes ClassWallet as its official financial management system. Once approved by the Arizona Department of Education, parents receive an online digital wallet where quarterly funding allocations are deposited. Parents use the ClassWallet Direct Pay feature to authorize payments to registered education providers without out-of-pocket costs.

Platform: ClassWallet
📅

Disbursements & Invoicing

Disbursements: Quarterly allocations directly into ClassWallet accounts

Invoicing: Direct Pay vendor invoicing with itemized receipts through ClassWallet

DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST

Typically Requested Verification Documents

State guidelines require verification of legal residency and student school eligibility. Prepare the following records prior to beginning your application:

  • Proof of Arizona physical residency (utility bill, mortgage statement, or residential lease)
  • Student certified birth certificate or state-recognized legal identification
  • Signed ESA Parent Contract with the Arizona Department of Education
  • Official withdrawal confirmation from the previous public or charter school (if transitioning)

STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS

How Families Apply and Authorize Providers

  1. Step 1: Submit your student's electronic application through the ADE ESA portal at AZEdu.gov/esa.
  2. Step 2: Sign the electronic agreement upon receiving official acceptance from the Arizona Department of Education.
  3. Step 3: Activate your parent ClassWallet digital wallet account when notification arrives from the state.
  4. Step 4: Verify your quarterly funding allocation and connect with approved education service providers via ClassWallet Direct Pay.

HELPFUL PROGRAM QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions About Empowerment Scholarship Account (ESA)

Can any Arizona K-12 student apply for an ESA?

Yes. Arizona provides universal eligibility for all kindergarten through 12th-grade students residing in the state, provided the student does not attend a public school district or charter school full-time while receiving ESA funding.

How does ClassWallet Direct Pay protect families?

ClassWallet Direct Pay allows the state to send payments directly to authorized educational providers. Parents review and approve every itemized invoice digitally without ever spending personal money up front.

Are ESA funds considered taxable income for the family?

Under Arizona state law, ESA scholarship funds disbursed for qualified educational expenses are not considered taxable personal income.

What happens if funds are not entirely spent by the end of the school year?

Unused funds in an active Arizona ESA account roll over into subsequent school quarters and academic years, allowing families to plan for future educational milestones.

Does Chanak Foundation charge for orientation regarding Arizona ESA?

No. Chanak Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit public charity providing 100% free informational guidance to help families understand state funding rules and documentation.
