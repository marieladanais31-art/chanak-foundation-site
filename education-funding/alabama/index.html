OFFICIAL PROGRAM OPERATION

How the The CHOOSE Act of 2024 Program Works

The Alabama CHOOSE Act provides refundable tax credits deposited into Education Savings Accounts for eligible students to fund tuition, curriculum, instructional support, and educational services.

🏛️

Administering Agency

Alabama Department of Revenue & State Department of Education. Governed by: Alabama Act 2024-85 (Creating Hope and Opportunity for Our Students' Education Act).

Visit Official State Portal ↗
💳

Digital Platform

Alabama partners with ClassWallet to provide a secure digital environment where parents manage their state ESA allocations and approve vendor payments.

Platform: ClassWallet
📅

Disbursements & Invoicing

Disbursements: State refundable tax credit/ESA distributions via ClassWallet

Invoicing: Direct Pay vendor approval by the Alabama Department of Revenue

DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST

Typically Requested Verification Documents

State guidelines require verification of legal residency and student school eligibility. Prepare the following records prior to beginning your application:

  • Alabama state residency documentation (state tax return, utility statement)
  • Student age verification (birth certificate)
  • Federal adjusted gross income verification
  • School choice participation commitment

STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS

How Families Apply and Authorize Providers

  1. Step 1: Create a parent profile on the Alabama Department of Revenue / ClassWallet portal.
  2. Step 2: Complete the annual application with household verification.
  3. Step 3: Confirm ESA account setup on ClassWallet once approved.
  4. Step 4: Authorize service provider payments via ClassWallet Direct Pay.

HELPFUL PROGRAM QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions About The CHOOSE Act of 2024

When does the Alabama CHOOSE Act take full effect?

The program begins rolling out for eligible lower-income families and students with special needs before expanding towards universal access across Alabama.

What wallet platform handles payments in Alabama?

ClassWallet is the designated provider for Alabama CHOOSE Act account management.

Can homeschool families participate in Alabama CHOOSE?

Yes, eligible homeschool and non-public school students may utilize the program for approved educational expenses under state rules.

Does a family have to pay vendors directly?

Through ClassWallet Direct Pay, payments are handled digitally from the state account to approved vendors without parent out-of-pocket payment.

What role does Chanak Foundation play?

Chanak Foundation offers free educational advisory to help Alabama families understand their options.
