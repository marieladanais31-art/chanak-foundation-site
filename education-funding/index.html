TECHNOLOGY & FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT

How Digital Platforms Work (ClassWallet, Odyssey, Step Up)

States utilize audited digital wallet systems to disburse education funds securely without direct cash payments to families:

ClassWallet

Administered in Arizona, Alabama, South Carolina, and New Hampshire. Empowers parents to pay registered providers via Direct Pay with no out-of-pocket personal expenditure.

Odyssey

Used in Utah and Wyoming. Manages the official digital marketplace where Qualified Vendors submit invoices for parent approval and state reimbursement.

EMA (Step Up for Students)

Florida official portal for PEP and FES-UA scholarships. Manages student accounts, verified provider catalogs, and electronic fee disbursements.
