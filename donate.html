🚨 Immediate Disaster Relief

Emergency Mission: Rebuilding Churubé, Coclé (Panama)

Following the October 2026 earthquake, the local school collapsed and 5 farming families lost their roofs. We are providing immediate galvanized roofing, solar study desks, and backpacks for 35 rural children.

Our Three Philanthropic Pillars

Where Your Generosity Creates Lasting Change

Chanak Foundation concentrates 100% of charitable gifts into three Candid-aligned impact programs with auditable milestone tracking.

🎓

Academic Scholarships

Need-based aid for displaced and low-income K-12 students across Panama, the U.S. and partner regions, covering textbooks, diagnostic assessment, and accredited credentials.

Learn about Scholarships →

📖

Christian Educator Mentorship

Pedagogical coaching in character formation, mastery learning, and orientation on state Education Savings Accounts (ESA) for educators serving vulnerable communities.

Learn about Educator Training →

🤝

Community Learning Hubs

Physical & digital study spaces, adult functional literacy, and bilingual adolescent life skills cohorts in Florida (Tampa Bay / St. Pete) and rural centers.

Learn about Community Hubs →
