Local Visionary Partners

A Multicultural Network Rooted in Local Communities

Our local visionary partners bring together lived experience from Latin America and Spain, creating trusted bridges with families and communities. The network is led by founders Mariela (Panama) and Elias (Spain), alongside multicultural couples serving locally across Spain.

Torrevieja · Mexico + Cuba

Mary Claudia García Jiménez & Henry Granda

Claudia, from Mexico, and Henry, from Cuba, bring a bicultural family perspective to Torrevieja. As local visionary partners, they help connect families, community spaces and educational support around belonging, character and opportunity for children and youth.

Jávea · Peru + Madrid

Medalith Rosales & Juan Carlos González

Medalith, from Peru, and Juan Carlos, from Madrid, strengthen the Foundation's local presence in Jávea by combining Latin American and Spanish perspectives. Their role is to build trust with families and help develop sustainable community-based learning pathways.

Vinaròs · Venezuela

Dayana Trillo & Mario Artis

Dayana and Mario, from Venezuela, contribute the perspective of immigrant and multicultural families building a future in Spain. In Vinaròs they support community connection, family engagement and accessible educational opportunities for young people.

Tarragona · Argentina + Catalonia

Thiare Urzua & Sergio Masía

Thiare, from Argentina, and Sergio, from Catalonia, represent the cross-cultural collaboration at the heart of the network. From Tarragona, they help strengthen local partnerships, mentoring pathways and community participation around children, youth and families.
