Our Mission Programs

Empowering Students, Youth, and Adults Through Purpose-Driven Education

Chanak Foundation develops practical, values-informed initiatives that support youth development, empower adult learning, provide academic scholarships, and equip families. As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit initiative, we focus on educational continuity, character formation, and community collaboration.

Flagship Youth Pathway

Life Skills & Leadership Development (Ages 10–18)

We don't just form students—we form tomorrow's leaders. Our comprehensive Life Skills curriculum provides intentional mentorship, emotional intelligence, ethical decision-making, and vocational clarity to guide youth from early adolescence into adulthood.

Seedling (Ages 10–12)

Identity, character foundations, emotional regulation, and first community service initiatives.

Explorer (Ages 12–14)

Vocational discovery, purpose formula, teamwork, and healthy interpersonal relationship habits.

Builder (Ages 14–16)

Real-world leadership projects, financial literacy, critical thinking, and public communication skills.

Launch (Ages 16–18)

Capstone project, university portfolio building, SAT & academic readiness, and career mentorship.

Lifelong Learning & Empowerment

Adult Education & Family Empowerment

Education doesn't stop at adolescence. Strong families require empowered parents and adults equipped for today's economic and cultural challenges. Chanak Foundation offers accessible community courses for adults to foster personal, professional, and spiritual development.

Parenting & Family Guidance

Workshops on adolescent development, positive discipline, digital safety, and creating a supportive learning environment at home.

Digital Literacy & Career Skills

Practical instruction in computer skills, cloud tools, digital communication, and remote productivity for adults entering or transitioning in the workforce.

Language & Communication

Bilingual ESL and conversational English sessions to help immigrant and Spanish-speaking parents engage fully in their local communities.

Community Leadership & Service

Training local volunteer leaders to manage community initiatives, church hubs, and neighborhood outreach programs.

Academic Inclusion & Mobility

Dual Diploma International Scholarship Fund

Our Dual Diploma scholarship program reduces financial barriers for talented students in Spanish-speaking communities to simultaneously complete the American High School Diploma alongside their local schooling.

Through philanthropic grants and donor support, students receive subsidized tuition, live bilingual tutoring, academic advising, and official US accreditation that opens doors to universities worldwide.

  • Dual Official Credential: Local national diploma plus accredited US High School Diploma.
  • Full Bilingual Immersion: Academic curriculum delivered in 100% English with dedicated faculty.
  • Need- and Merit-Based Aid: Subsidized scholarship access for qualifying families (subject to available foundation funds and donor sponsorship).
  • University Pathway: Direct preparation for international higher education admissions.

Community Hubs & Pods

Open Educational Tool

Free Diagnostic Assessment & Academic Orientation

Every child learns differently. Our comprehensive diagnostic evaluation provides families with a clear, objective analysis of their student's cognitive strengths, reading comprehension, English language proficiency, and learning style.

This service is provided 100% free of charge as a community service of Chanak Foundation, accompanied by a personalized consultation session with our educational specialists.

  • Comprehensive assessment for students ages 6 to 18
  • Detailed pedagogical report for parents and educators
  • Personalized roadmap and curriculum recommendations
