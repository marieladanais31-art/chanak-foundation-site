PROGRAM PORTFOLIO

Three Official Programs

1. Youth Life Skills & Global Action Circles

Youth development focused on purpose, resilience, leadership, financial literacy, career readiness and community action.

2. Community Learning Hubs & Adult Literacy

Digital inclusion, educational continuity, adult learning and community-based access in underserved or remote locations.

3. Dual Diploma & Academic Scholarship Fund

Need-based scholarship support for eligible students facing financial barriers to accredited secondary education pathways.

Separation from Educational Operations

Chanak Foundation is the philanthropic and social-impact portal of CHANAK TRAINUP EDUCATION, INC. Its purpose is fundraising, charitable program delivery, scholarships, community partnerships and public accountability.

Chanak Academy's educational platform, curriculum, LMS/SIS infrastructure and fee-based educational services are presented separately at the Academy portal. Foundation grants and charitable appeals are not presented as commercial enrollment offers.

PARTNERSHIP PRINCIPLES

Community-Centered and Purpose-Driven

Our roots are informed by Christian values of service, dignity and stewardship. Programs funded through the Foundation are designed around community need and equitable educational access; participation is not conditioned on religious affiliation.
