PROGRAM PORTFOLIO

Three Official Programs

1. Youth Life Skills & Global Action Circles

Character and virtue development for ages 10–18, built through mentoring, teamwork and real community action rather than classroom theory alone — practicing moral, performance, civic and intellectual virtues across a four-stage pathway.

2. Community Learning Hubs & Adult Literacy

Digital inclusion, educational continuity, adult learning and community-based access in underserved or remote locations.

3. Dual Diploma & Academic Scholarship Fund

Need-based scholarship support for eligible students in the United States and internationally who face financial barriers to accredited secondary education pathways. Depending on grant restrictions and available funds, applicants may include students in the U.S., Spain, Panama, Mexico, Venezuela and other communities served by the Foundation.

FUNDING USE

What Philanthropic Funds Support

Scholarships

Restricted support for qualified students selected under scholarship eligibility and financial-need criteria. Geographic eligibility follows the requirements of each grant or donor restriction.

Community Infrastructure

Learning devices, connectivity, classroom equipment, educational materials and local hub activation.

Program Delivery & Measurement

Training, mentoring, curriculum implementation, community coordination, safeguarding and outcome measurement. Chanak Foundation is open to external evaluation and academic partnerships that strengthen the evidence base of its programs, including its character-development model.

U.S.-Based Administration, Flexible Geographic Impact

CHANAK TRAINUP EDUCATION, INC. is a U.S.-based 501(c)(3) public charity. The Foundation can structure restricted projects for U.S.-based beneficiaries or for approved international cohorts, depending on the geographic rules of each funder.

For grants that require U.S. service delivery, funds are restricted to eligible U.S. activities and beneficiaries. For funders that permit international impact, the Foundation can support qualified students and community programs across additional countries.

Separation from Educational Operations

Chanak Foundation is the philanthropic and social-impact portal of CHANAK TRAINUP EDUCATION, INC. Its purpose is fundraising, charitable program delivery, scholarships, community partnerships and public accountability.

Chanak Academy's educational platform, curriculum, LMS/SIS infrastructure and fee-based educational services are presented separately at the Academy portal. Foundation grants and charitable appeals are not presented as commercial enrollment offers.
