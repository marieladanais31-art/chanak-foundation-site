Ground-Level Intelligence

What Happened in Churubé of Natá

On the morning of the earthquake, seismic shocks centered in central Panama tore through the agrarian community of Churubé, located in the El Caño subdivision of Natá de los Caballeros, Coclé Province.

While urban areas experienced limited damage, rural adobe, timber, and non-reinforced masonry homes suffered catastrophic fractures. The C.E.B.G. Simeón Valderrama—the sole educational institution serving the community’s elementary children—was deemed structurally condemned. Five farming families found their galvanized roofs caved in and outer walls cracked beyond repair.

📍
Why Chanak Foundation: Through our established educational missions in Panama, our local coordinators are based right in Coclé. We are cutting through bureaucratic delays by deploying direct materials and setting up temporary modular learning stations in the families' yards.
