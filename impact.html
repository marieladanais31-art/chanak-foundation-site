IMPACT & ACCOUNTABILITY

Measuring Progress Through Access, Participation and Community Capacity

Chanak Foundation organizes its charitable work around three official programs. We track progress through participation, continuity, family engagement, youth development, scholarship access and the capacity of local partners to sustain learning opportunities over time.

OUR THREE OFFICIAL PROGRAMS

One Measurement Framework Across Three Lines of Impact

Youth Life Skills & Global Action Circles

What we track: participation, completion, mentor engagement, student self-reflection, project and service delivery, responsibility and leadership practice, perseverance, sense of purpose and belonging, and future-readiness milestones, checked at baseline, midpoint and end-of-program.

Community Learning Hubs & Adult Literacy

What we track: learners reached, hours of access, digital participation, adult learning engagement, family participation, educational continuity and locally sustained hub activity.

Dual Diploma & Academic Scholarship Fund

What we track: scholarship applications, verified need, awards funded, student persistence, academic advising, pathway completion and responsible use of restricted scholarship funds.

REAL-WORLD EVIDENCE

We Measure More Than Attendance

Program participation matters, but durable impact requires evidence that families remain engaged, young people build practical capacity, learners continue progressing and community partners can sustain access beyond a single event.

  • Participation and program completion
  • Family and mentor feedback
  • Educational continuity and persistence
  • Project, portfolio or service evidence
  • Digital literacy and practical skills participation
  • Local partner capacity and hub continuity
  • Scholarship stewardship and student progress

As the program expands, Chanak Foundation intends to strengthen its evaluation framework through validated measures, mentor observations, participant reflection and longitudinal follow-up, and is interested in external evaluation and academic partnerships to strengthen the evidence base of its character-development model.

COMMUNITY LEARNING HUBS

Territory Matters

In geographically isolated communities, educational access is shaped by transport, connectivity, distance and local infrastructure. Our Hub model is designed around those realities rather than assuming that learners can simply travel to an existing service.

In Panama, field experience and community relationships inform the development of locally accessible learning options for rural and coastal communities. The Foundation uses territorial evidence to define equipment needs, connectivity priorities, delivery schedules and realistic participation targets.

YOUTH DEVELOPMENT

Practical Skills Become Visible Through Action

Life Skills is designed to move beyond classroom discussion. Project-based learning, teamwork, service, practical responsibility and mentoring provide observable evidence of growth in confidence, collaboration, problem-solving and leadership practice.

Activities such as community projects, STEM challenges, entrepreneurship exercises, gardening, arts and service experiences can be documented in student portfolios and mentor observations.

LONG-TERM OUTCOMES

From Access to Continuity

Our long-term goal is not simply to create activities. It is to help learners remain connected to education, strengthen family participation, expand practical opportunity and build community structures capable of supporting future cohorts.

As programs mature, the Foundation will publish program-level targets and annual results so funders and community partners can compare intended outcomes with actual delivery.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Responsible Stewardship of Philanthropic Support

Restricted Purpose

Program-specific funding is directed to the charitable purpose for which it was awarded, including scholarships, equipment, connectivity and program delivery.

Documented Delivery

Participation, program activities, partner contributions and material outputs are documented as programs are implemented.

Transparent Reporting

Chanak Foundation is building a reporting framework that links program budgets, outputs and outcomes for donors and institutional funders.
