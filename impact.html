Community Outcomes

Responsible, Community-Centered Impact

Chanak Foundation is committed to educational continuity, family partnership, and leadership growth through practical, mission-aligned nonprofit programming.

Our Core Areas of Impact

Youth Life Skills & Leadership

Guiding adolescents through a 4-stage formation pathway (Seedling, Explorer, Builder, Launch) focused on character, purpose, and future readiness.

Adult Education & Parenting

Equipping parents with adolescent communication tools, digital literacy, ESL conversational English, and workforce skills.

Dual Diploma Scholarships

Reducing financial barriers for talented youth to obtain the accredited American High School Diploma alongside local schooling.

Church-Based Learning Hubs

Activating local church spaces as safe, accessible community learning hubs on Spain's Costa Blanca and beyond.

Free Diagnostic Assessments

Providing open-access cognitive, reading, and language evaluations to help families design individualized learning pathways.

Family Strengthening

Connecting home routines and educational support to protect youth against isolation, bullying, and academic disruption.

How We Measure Progress

Chanak Foundation evaluates progress through student participation, academic continuity, family feedback, mentor check-ins, and the long-term development of local learning hubs.

Our Long-Term Vision

We are building a durable model of community education in which churches, families, and mission partners collaborate to help youth develop character, leadership, and purpose.
