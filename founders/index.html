OUR FOUNDERS

Faith, education and service, from Panama to Spain and Florida

Chanak comes from the Hebrew chanak: to dedicate, to initiate, to consecrate. It is the name of a calling shared by the people who started this work.

Founder and Head of School

Why Chanak

Our founders saw that many families and communities have the calling and the people to educate, but lack a system. Chanak offers structure, training and accreditation so that small schools can have an excellent education rooted in faith, wherever they are.

«Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.» Proverbs 22:6

A team and a network

The founders lead a multicultural team in Florida, Spain and Panama. Chanak International Academy, the operating school affiliated with the nonprofit, is accredited by MSA-CESS (Kindergarten–Grade 12) and registered with the Florida Department of Education (#134620).
