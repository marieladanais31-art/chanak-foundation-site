COMMUNITY-CENTERED CHARITABLE IMPACT

Expanding Educational Access and Opportunity for Families and Youth

Chanak TrainUp Education, Inc. is a U.S. 501(c)(3) public charity dedicated to strengthening educational pathways where resources are limited. Through community educator mentoring, youth workforce readiness frameworks, and bilingual family guidance, we help communities build durable learning capacity rooted in character, dignity, and service.

OUR THREE OFFICIAL INITIATIVES

Structured for Transparent Community Impact

Our program structure concentrates charitable resources in three areas of measurable community benefit:

Educator Mentorship

Community Guide & Educator Training

Equipping community educators and parents with diagnostic, mastery-based instruction methods and character-driven pedagogy. Preparing trained Guides to support students who require tailored pacing, bilingual learning, and close academic tracking.

Status: Active charitable training workshops and bilingual curriculum orientation.
Career & Workforce

Youth Workforce Readiness

A structured four-week hybrid onboarding curriculum for high school youth (ages 16–18):

  • Week 1: Strengths identification & first industry-standard résumé.
  • Week 2: Workplace communication, business etiquette & feedback.
  • Week 3: Paycheck literacy (gross vs. net, taxes) & budgeting.
  • Week 4: Job search strategies, mock interviews & career plan.
Status: Proposed grant-funded pilot initiative; curriculum designed; seeking funding & community partners.
Family Access

Educational Access & Family Resources

Bilingual family guidance and informational support navigating state school choice and Education Savings Account (ESA) programs across Arizona, Florida, South Carolina, and New Hampshire. 100% free guidance ensuring families access state funds directly through authorized portals.

Status: Active community guidance service. Visit State Funding Hub →

GOVERNANCE & ACCOUNTABILITY

Distinct Institutional Roles

Chanak TrainUp Education, Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN: 36-5154011). The Foundation oversees charitable programming, philanthropic grant administration, community educator mentoring, need-based scholarship access, and family orientation services.

Chanak International Academy is the operating school affiliated with the nonprofit, administering student admissions, accredited curriculum delivery, grade transcripts, and high school diploma conferral.

For families seeking formal school admissions, course catalogs, or official student record requests, visit Chanak International Academy (chanakacademy.org).

Educational Assistance in Panama

Chanak TrainUp Education, Inc. has formulated and submitted grant proposals seeking philanthropic support to expand educational assistance, learning kits, and family guidance in rural and peri-urban Panama.

Project Status: Proposed initiative; grant funding currently sought.
