MICROSCHOOL · FLORIDA

Microschool Florida: learning close to home in Tampa Bay

A small cohort, a trained Guide and a safe space hosted by a church, community center or parent co-op. Chanak TrainUp Education, Inc. is based in St. Petersburg, Florida, and this is where our network begins.

Status: Funding sought. In design and pilot preparation. No cohort locations are open yet; they will be announced as host agreements and facility approvals are completed.

Who it is for

Families

Parents who want rigorous, personalized, values-aligned learning for their children between home learning and a large school.

Churches and community centers

Hosts with a safe, approved space who want to serve families in their neighborhood.

Parent co-ops

Groups of families that already learn together and need structure, trained leadership and academic tracking.

How a Florida microschool opens

  1. 1

    Host agreement

    A written agreement with the hosting church, center or co-op that sets roles, space use and safety responsibilities.

  2. 2

    Facility review

    The space is reviewed against municipal and state requirements before any student attends.

  3. 3

    Guide training

    The Guide completes Chanak's mentoring and mastery-learning training, with bilingual tools.

  4. 4

    Diagnostic start

    Every student begins with a diagnostic assessment so learning starts exactly where the student is.

  5. 5

    Launch and follow-up

    The cohort starts small, with regular progress reports for families and oversight from Chanak.

What the Foundation provides

Chanak Foundation supplies Guide training frameworks, bilingual learning kits and family orientation resources. Hosts provide the safe, approved physical facility.

Official academic records and diploma pathways for enrolled students are administered by Chanak International Academy, accredited by MSA-CESS (K–12) and registered with the Florida Department of Education (#134620).

Families in Florida may be able to use state education funds for services that Chanak is approved to provide. This is subject to each program's approval. See our free orientation at the State Funding hub.
