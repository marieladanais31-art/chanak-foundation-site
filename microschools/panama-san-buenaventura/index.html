MICROSCHOOL · PANAMA

Microschool Panama: secondary education where roads end

In mountain and rural communities, finishing secondary school can mean hours of travel that families cannot afford. The San Buenaventura microschool is designed to bring quality secondary education to students who live far from a school.

Status: Funding sought. Proposed initiative in community assessment. Site details are confirmed with community leaders and Panama's education authorities before any cohort starts.

Designed for hard-to-reach places

Solar power

Reliable electricity from solar energy, so lessons do not depend on the grid.

Offline-first learning

Lessons, assessments and tools that work without internet and sync when a connection is available.

Trained local Guides

A Guide from or near the community, trained in mastery learning and mentoring, with regular support visits.

Secondary-level focus

Grades that families struggle most to keep their children in, with a clear path to complete secondary school.

How we will start

  1. 1

    Listen first

    Meetings with families, community leaders and local educators to confirm needs and the right location.

  2. 2

    Authorizations

    Coordination with Panama's education authorities on how the program operates.

  3. 3

    Prepare the space and kit

    A safe, solar-powered room and a portable learning kit.

  4. 4

    Train and begin

    Guide training, diagnostic assessments and a small first cohort, with progress shared with families.

Why Panama

Panama is the home of our founder's pioneering educational work in community and off-campus learning. This microschool provides a proven, mastery-based secondary learning model so rural teenagers have a direct, accredited path to complete high school without leaving their communities.

Help bring secondary education to San Buenaventura

Designated gifts pay for Guide training, a solar-powered learning space, learning kits and need-based scholarships.
