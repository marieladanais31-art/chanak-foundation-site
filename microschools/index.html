COMMUNITY MICROSCHOOL NETWORK

Small schools, close to home, rooted in faith and excellence

Chanak TrainUp Education, Inc. is building a network of community microschools: small, mastery-based cohorts guided in person by a trained Guide, hosted by churches, community centers and local partners. We are raising seed funding for three starting points: Florida, Tarragona and the mountains of Panama.

Status: Funding sought. These are planned projects in design and pilot preparation; sites, schedules and cohorts are confirmed only as host agreements and approvals are finalized.

What is different about a Chanak microschool

Mastery before moving on

Students advance when they truly master a skill, not because the calendar says so. A Guide reteaches immediately when a concept does not land.

Small, multi-age cohorts

Typically 8 to 15 students, so the Guide knows every learner deeply and peers learn collaboration and leadership.

Bilingual diagnostics

Initial and cycle-based diagnostic assessments in English and Spanish show each student's exact baseline and progress.

A foundation of faith

Character formation and a biblical worldview are part of everyday learning, never an add-on.

Academic integrity and oversight

Community microschools meet in locally hosted spaces. Official academic records, diagnostic frameworks and diploma pathways are administered through Chanak International Academy, the operating school affiliated with the nonprofit, accredited by MSA-CESS (Kindergarten–Grade 12) and registered with the Florida Department of Education (#134620).

Compliance with each country's school regulations is confirmed with local authorities before any site opens. Credit acceptance always depends on the receiving institution.
