COMMUNITY-BASED LEARNING MODELS

Community Microschools: Small-Group Learning with Dedicated Mentorship

A community microschool provides an encouraging middle option between learning entirely at home and a conventional large-school setting. Students learn in small cohorts guided in person by a trained community Guide, progressing according to demonstrated mastery within a safe, values-aligned peer community.

PEDAGOGICAL FRAMEWORK

Core Principles of the Chanak Microschool Model

Designed to meet the needs of bilingual and working-class families seeking rigorous, personalized instruction close to home.

Mastery-Based Progression

Learners advance when they truly master a skill, not merely because a calendar week has passed. If a student struggles with a concept, the Guide reteaches it right away using alternate explanations rather than moving ahead prematurely.

Compact Multi-Age Cohorts

Small groups (typically 8 to 15 students) allow the Guide to know each learner deeply. Multi-age peer dynamics encourage collaboration, healthy socialization, mutual respect, and leadership development.

Bilingual Diagnostic Assessments

Initial and cycle-based diagnostic assessments identify precise academic baselines, providing teachers and parents with objective skill-by-skill progress tracking in both English and Spanish.

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY & OVERSIGHT

Academic Enrollment & Records Oversight

While community microschools meet locally in spaces hosted by community partners or family associations, all official academic records, diagnostic testing frameworks, student transcripts, and high school diploma pathways are maintained and administered through our registered operating school:

Chanak International Academy

Florida-registered private K–12 school · Florida Department of Education (FLDOE) School Code: #134620. Operating as an educational program of Chanak TrainUp Education, Inc.

This institutional structure ensures that community learning retains certified academic validity, compliant state reporting, and seamless credit transferability for participating students.

Role of Community & Church Partners

Chanak Foundation collaborates with local churches, community centers, and parent co-ops who wish to host a microschool environment. Partners provide safe, approved physical facilities, while the Foundation supplies Guide training frameworks, bilingual learning kits, and family orientation resources.

  • Safe, certified learning spaces with reliable connectivity.
  • Parent workshops on mastery learning and home support.
  • Trained bilingual Guides focused on academic excellence.
