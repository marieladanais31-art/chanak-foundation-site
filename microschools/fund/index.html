MICROSCHOOL SEED FUND

Plant the first microschools

Every network begins with a few first schools. Your tax-deductible gift to the Microschool Seed Fund helps open the first community microschools in Florida, Tarragona and Panama, and pilot a STEM virtual-reality lab for remote communities.

Status: Funding sought for planned projects. Contributions are restricted to this purpose.

What your gift pays for

Guide training

Mentoring and mastery-learning training for the Guides who lead each microschool.

Learning kits and diagnostics

Bilingual learning materials and diagnostic assessments for every student.

Safe, connected spaces

Safety adaptation of host spaces, connectivity and, in remote areas, solar power.

Need-based scholarships

Support for students whose families cannot cover costs.

STEM virtual-reality pilot

A first portable lab for a remote community.

Reporting and evaluation

Tracking of outcomes and open reports to donors.

Accountability

Chanak TrainUp Education, Inc. is a U.S. 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 36-5154011). Gifts to the Microschool Seed Fund are restricted to the purposes above. If a designated project cannot proceed, Chanak will use the gift for the closest similar charitable purpose. We will report on how funds are used.

No goods or services are provided in exchange for your donation. Stripe: Chanak TrainUp Education, Inc. issues your receipt. PayPal Giving Fund: PayPal Giving Fund issues its own receipt.

A COPY OF THE OFFICIAL REGISTRATION AND FINANCIAL INFORMATION MAY BE OBTAINED FROM THE DIVISION OF CONSUMER SERVICES BY CALLING TOLL-FREE WITHIN THE STATE (1-800-435-7352) OR VISITING WWW.FLORIDACONSUMERHELP.COM. REGISTRATION DOES NOT IMPLY ENDORSEMENT, APPROVAL, OR RECOMMENDATION BY THE STATE. FLORIDA REGISTRATION #CH84302.
