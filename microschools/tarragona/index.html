MICROSCHOOL · TARRAGONA, SPAIN

Microschool Tarragona: a community school with a local partner

In Tarragona we work hand in hand with Asociación Cristiana EducaFe, a local Christian association that already serves families and teenagers in the area. Together we are preparing a community microschool with the same mastery-based model.

Status: Funding sought. In design and preparation with our local partner. The legal framework for each site in Spain is confirmed with the partner and the competent education authority before opening.

Support the Tarragona microschoolMeet our local partner

Our local partner

Asociación Cristiana EducaFe

A Christian association based in Mont-roig del Camp, Tarragona, offering educational resources, family and vocational guidance, and community programs.

Projecte Brúixola

EducaFe's free socio-educational program for adolescents aged 12 to 18 at risk of leaving school, open regardless of beliefs, origin or income.

Family network

Home-educating and school-seeking families who want structure, community and trained mentors close by.

What a Tarragona microschool looks like

Trilingual learning

Spanish, Catalan and English in daily learning, with local language, history and geography respected.

Mastery and character

Mastery-based progress and character formation guided by a trained Guide.

Rooted in the community

Learning that connects to the land, the families and the church community around it.

Path to opening

  1. 1

    Partner agreement

    Clear roles between Chanak and the local partner.

  2. 2

    Legal and space review

    Confirmation of the applicable Spanish framework and of a suitable, safe space.

  3. 3

    Guide training

    A locally based Guide trained in Chanak's mentoring and mastery model.

  4. 4

    Pilot cohort

    A small pilot group, measured with diagnostics and reported to families.

The first priority is the people: the Guide's training, the learning kits and a safe space. Seed funding makes that possible.
